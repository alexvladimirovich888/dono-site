What is dono?

dono is a multichain platform that turns memecoin creator fees into support for livestreamers. Solana is live today, with more chains coming soon. The current launch route pairs a Pump.fun token with a Twitch recipient. Trading generates eligible creator fees, and dono routes the fees it receives toward streamer payouts and the platform token.

80% for streamer payouts. 20% for $DONO buybacks and burns.

The split applies to creator fees received by dono. Launchpad protocol fees and liquidity fees are separate.

The catalog lists confirmed token launches and recorded financial activity. A recipient listing does not imply affiliation or endorsement. SolCard funds the gifts. A donation is marked complete only after its destination and payment evidence have been verified.

Launch a token

Connect your Solana wallet, add your token name, ticker and artwork, and verify a Twitch channel. Pump.fun on Solana is the supported launch route. More launchpads and streamer platforms are coming soon.

  1. Select Launch. Continue from your completed token form.
  2. Choose an optional dev buy. Enter a SOL amount or leave it blank to launch without buying. Any purchased tokens go to your connected wallet.
  3. Confirm in your wallet. dono uploads your artwork and prepares the transaction automatically, then opens your wallet for approval. Creation and any dev buy happen together in one transaction.

The total estimate includes the optional buy, network fees, account creation and the dedicated creator-wallet reserve. The token appears only after Solana confirms its creation. The form displays current launch availability.

Once a signed launch has been submitted, dono keeps checking its confirmation even if you close the page. Confirmation and catalog refreshes can take time. Explore searches the complete confirmed catalog; use Load more to browse additional tokens.

Streamer payouts

Twitch is the current gifting destination. Kick is coming soon. Each token keeps its own recipient allocation. Creator-fee payouts require at least $50 USD available after funding and payment costs. New automated gift checkouts also use a $50 minimum. Completed gifts are recorded at the actual amount charged, including reviewed purchases below $50. Smaller unspent balances stay assigned to the token.

How long until fees become a dono?

There is no fixed daily payout time. Trading activity determines how quickly eligible fees build up: an active token can reach the thresholds sooner, while a quieter token may stay below them indefinitely. Fees are claimed once at least $50 is eligible. After the 80 / 20 split, the streamer allocation must separately cover costs and the $50 payout minimum. A $50 claim leaves $40 for the streamer before costs, so it needs more fees before a payout.

Eligible fees are checked roughly every 30 seconds. A payout may need several scheduled checks, plus time for chain confirmation, SolCard credit, checkout, and purchase verification. Traffic and other scheduled payouts can make the interval longer or shorter. This is not a promise of a gift within 30 seconds. Completed creator-fee gifts are published in Donos with their funding transaction and confirmed purchase amount. A new pending payout does not change the status of gifts already sent.

How the $50 minimum works

For automated checkout, the minimum is the total gift purchase in USD, not the price of one subscription. The number of whole subs depends on the current checkout price, currency, and tax. The checkout total, including applicable tax, must be at least $50 and fit within the funded allowance; if it cannot meet both conditions, the funds wait. Any unspent balance stays assigned to that token.

If the streamer is offline

Eligible fees can still be claimed when claiming is enabled. New card funding and gifts wait until the selected streamer is verified live. Routine live-status checks run about every 30 minutes, with a fresh check before funding or checkout. An unavailable or uncertain live status also keeps the funds held.

If the card was already funded, that payment's credit remains assigned to the same token and recipient while they are offline. A submitted or uncertain payment continues through reconciliation; coming back online does not authorize a duplicate purchase.

The automated gifting workflow checks the verified channel and live status before using the funded SolCard. The ledger records the actual spend and gift units from confirmed payment evidence. Gifted subscriptions count as support spending, not the streamer's net cash earnings.

Completion requires confirmed funding and confirmation that the gift was delivered. A public invoice is optional; when one is not shared, the recorded purchase still appears in Donos with its recipient, amount and funding transaction. A top-up alone never counts as a donation. If the outcome is uncertain, the payment stays reserved while the system reconciles the existing attempt. A possible charge must be resolved before another gift can be sent.

Being selected is not an endorsement.

Streamer names and photographs belong to real public creators. Their appearance does not imply affiliation, consent to a token launch, or endorsement. Completed payments have both funding evidence and a verified donation receipt.

The 80 / 20 split

For every $100 of creator fees received by dono, $80 is allocated to streamer payouts and $20 is allocated to buying and burning the official platform token. This is an allocation example, not a promised trading return.

Creator fees are only one part of the fees charged by a launchpad. Rates can depend on the token, launch version, pool, and quote asset. We will use the actual confirmed amounts received instead of estimating payouts from trading volume.

The split uses integer base units. Any rounding remainder stays with the streamer allocation. SolCard funding, conversion, and payment costs are itemized against the streamer allocation. Claim transaction network fees are recorded separately as an operating treasury expense. The 20% buyback reserve stays separate. The payment budget is the remaining amount after those costs.

Claiming and sending each have a separate $50 USD threshold. Eligible unclaimed fees must reach $50 before claiming. The net streamer allocation must independently reach $50 before creating a payment; a $50 claim therefore does not immediately create a $50 donation.

A buyback is a purchase. A burn is a separate, confirmed supply-reduction step. Funds reserved for a buyback are not counted as tokens already burned. $DONO's production mint must be configured before buyback execution can be enabled.

Treasury & receipts

The treasury view separates fees claimed, streamer allocations, SolCard funding, reserved payment budgets, completed support spending, buyback reserves, and burns. Those balances describe different stages and must not be added together as if they were all new revenue.

  1. Earned → claimed. A chain receipt confirms the creator fees that reached dono.
  2. Allocated → funded. Record the 80 / 20 split, exchange rate, costs, confirmed Solana funding transaction, and SolCard credit reference.
  3. Reserved → sent. The payout worker checks the recipient, live status, reserved budget, and confirmed funding before checkout. A submitted payment is not yet proof of a completed gift.
  4. Confirmed → published. The system matches the amount, gift units, and donation confirmation or invoice to the payment. The paired funding transaction and gift evidence become one completed donation record.

Retries must reuse the same operation identity. An uncertain response needs reconciliation before another payment attempt. Public receipts should explain the route without exposing private bank or identity-verification details.

Payment integrations

SolCard · selected funding route

The route is Pump.fun creator fees → Solana treasury → SolCard funding → Twitch support. Each funding transfer must match a token allocation and an expected card credit. The adapter needs an approved account, deposit configuration, and reconciliation access before live operation. Credentials and card details never belong in public receipts.

SolCard funds the purchase; it does not send a gift to a username. Its institutional API and available account tools are configured for the sending account. API credentials alone do not provide an authenticated Twitch checkout session. Kick is coming soon.

SolCard institutional API · SolCard terms

Ledger agent

Attribute claims to tokens, apply the 80 / 20 split, itemize costs, reserve each payment budget once, and reconcile funding and gift receipts. Claim and payment thresholds are independently set to $50 USD. Uncertain transactions remain reserved until their outcomes are reconciled.

Automated gifting

When an automated payout route is enabled, the payment browser agent uses the verified recipient, channel link, reserved budget, and confirmed funding to complete checkout. Receipt pickup and payment reconciliation verify the result before the ledger publishes a completed donation.

Execution pauses if authentication expires, a payment challenge appears, prices change, or the recipient differs from the reserved payout. An ambiguous submission stays reserved until its outcome is confirmed, preventing duplicate gifts. The browser worker does not hold the treasury signing key.

Frequently asked questions

Can I launch a real token here today?

The launch page shows current availability. When enabled, connect your wallet, verify a Twitch recipient, review the costs and approve in your Solana wallet. A token is listed only after chain confirmation.

Does a streamer need to own crypto?

No crypto wallet is needed to receive a supported Twitch gift. Kick gifting is coming soon. dono funds the sending account through SolCard and verifies the channel, live status, and gift eligibility before automated checkout.

Does trading guarantee a donation?

No. The token must generate eligible creator fees that are actually routed to dono. Recipient eligibility, minimum payout amounts, provider availability, and successful settlement also matter.

How quickly are claimed fees sent as donos?

There is no guaranteed delivery time. Eligible fees are checked roughly every 30 seconds, but funding, live status, checkout and receipt verification take additional time. Confirmed gifts appear in Donos; any next payout is tracked separately.

Will dono send less than $50 or send while a streamer is offline?

Creator-fee gifts must total at least $50 USD after funding and payment costs. Smaller balances accumulate. Offline or unverified live status holds new funding and gifting; routine status checks run about every 30 minutes, with a fresh check before spending. Existing funds remain assigned to their token and recipient.

Is a stream alert the same as a donation?

No. An alert is a notification. A real payment needs a separate completed payment record and reconciliation. Alerts have their own retry and delivery status.

What happens if a payout cannot complete?

The ledger retains the recipient allocation and reserved budget. A failed or uncertain gift cannot count as paid. The system reconciles the existing payment attempt before another gift can be sent, preventing a possible duplicate charge.

Are the displayed streamers and tokens real?

The catalog contains confirmed launches and real public channel profiles. Unavailable market data is shown as a dash. Donation totals count completed support spending, not a promise about the streamer’s net earnings. A listing does not imply endorsement.

Is holding $DONO required?

No. Twenty percent of claimed creator fees is reserved for platform token buybacks and burns. A configured official mint and confirmed execution are required before reserves can count as spent or burned.

Live data & availability

Public token pages, donation totals and receipts come from the platform ledger. The catalog may be empty before the first confirmed launch. Missing market prices are shown as unavailable rather than estimated from invented trading activity.

Wallet connections, streamer lookup and launch availability depend on the current provider connections. A launch needs your explicit wallet approval. A saved or submitted intent is not a confirmed token, and a card top-up is not a completed donation.

Kick, TikTok, other chains, PONs and Four.meme are coming soon. Funding and gifting stay gated until the required account connections, balances and reconciliation checks are ready.
